Background

This is the interactive player-piano demonstrated at Balisage 2026 and Declarative Amsterdam 2026. It uses SaxonJS2, XSLT4(ish)*, SVG, MusicXML, some small amount of JavaScript code and the WebAudio API.

It is really meant to be a bit of fun, not the start of a musical career!

With the mouse you can press a key to play a note. There are controls for both the volume and the 'instrument' (tone) being played.

Musical Scores

You can also play one of a number of musical scores available from the drop-down list. For each at the bottom of the page you can see the original MusicXML, various 'compilations' of the score for purposes of playing and a graphical piano-roll.

The standard controls for play/pause/stop are provided and back/forward a bar is enabled when paused. The start & end measures can be changed. The tempo can be altered and the overall volume changed, using the obvious controls.

The scores have been written in multi-part form, such as left and right hand and voice. (Technically the score-partwise structure in MusicXML.) Each part can be suppressed if desired or the 'instrument' used to play that part altered. These changes only happen at the start of a bar, not within, and don't effect the keystrokes - only the audio.

If a score has lyrics, a 'laser display board' will appear at the top of the screen so you can sing along!

I currently don't have support for externally-loaded MusicXML scores - I'll experiment with this but make no guarantees.

Miscellaneous

The metronome swings only at the tempo when 'play' has been hit and the positioning of the balance weight isn't perhaps as accurate as it should be, though it is altered as the requested tempo changes. If you prefer your grand piano with an open lid, then please just click on it!

Very occasionally audio may stop - the Reset Audio button should do what it says on the tin.

My slides of my presentation at Declarative Amsterdam describing some of the architecture are available.

* The XSLT source is written in XSLT4 structures, with mostly XPath3 expressions. The source is translated into equivalent XSLT3 and then compile/exported into the SEF for SaxonJS2 using Saxon12 EE
